Q-Arm End EffectorA gripper and ordering software for warehouse parcels

A custom gripper that lets a Q arm pick up objects of different shapes and sizes and place them into parcels without losing its grip.

  • Oct 2 – Dec 3, 2025
  • Team Thurs-45
  • Q arm · N20 motor

Brief

Pick anything. Drop nothing.

The gripper closes from the motor’s rotation, stays inside the Q arm’s position and orientation tolerances, and needs less torque than the N20 motor can deliver. It pairs with software that lets clients queue and control robot tasks.

  • Lightweight
  • Within Q arm load limits
  • Few parts, few failure points
  • Fully manufacturable
  • Low torque

Design

From complicated to simple

  • Too complicated. A design we thought was strong got dropped for a simpler one.
  • Not enough grip. The material slipped, so we added rubber bands. It worked surprisingly well.
  • Small code bugs. Fixed by reviewing the code together.
